# The Breath and the Seal

Two rules make the halls different from anywhere else in Minecraft. **The Breath** changes how health
works. **The seal** changes what you can do to the world.

---

## The Breath

> *The dead took the breath of the living when the kingdoms went down, and the halls are still thick
> with it.*

### 1. Arriving fills you

The moment you fall in, you gain **ten extra hearts**: thirty in total. You watch the bar grow while
you're still drifting down. You fight the whole delve at thirty hearts.

### 2. Nothing heals you by itself

There is **no natural regeneration** below. A full hunger bar does nothing for your health. **Food is
only sprint fuel.**

### 3. The dead give it back

Putting down one of the dead **returns Breath to whoever landed the final blow**. You'll see heart
particles burst from you. There is no sound, because the bell owns all sound.

| Put down | Breath returned |
|---|---|
| a common dead | 1 heart |
| a marked dead | 1.5 hearts |
| a wight | 4 hearts |
| one of the Oathbound | 10 hearts |
| a king | all of it |

### 4. Potions and golden apples still work

They're worth more than ever, but each one heals a smaller share of a thirty-heart bar. They're a
tool, not the answer. Hold I's hoards always contain at least **two healing potions** between them,
so a player with nothing but the kit isn't locked out.

### Why this matters

This rule is what makes the game work. **Your health bar is your timer.**

- **Clearing a room** costs real minutes but **pays you in hearts**.
- **Robbing** a hoard saves real minutes but costs fights and wakes the hold.
- So a player in a hurry robs Hold I and leaves at half health with a full bag. A player with the
  evening clears their way down and reaches the Throne at full strength.

It also flips the usual dungeon instinct: **avoiding fights is how you run out.**

### The Breath goes home with you

Walk out, die, or get thrown out at dawn and the ten extra hearts leave you. You're back to a normal
body above.

## The seal: the halls are held

> *The halls are held exactly as they were the night they sank. Nothing there rusts, nothing there
> mends, and nothing there gives way to a pick.*

### What you can't do below

| Action | Below |
|---|---|
| Place any block | **refused** |
| Break any block | **refused**, except ore, decorated pots, and (planned) wardstones |
| Empty a bucket | refused |
| Use a bed | refused |
| Blow anything up | refused |
| Glide with an elytra | refused |
| Use a map | it renders **blank**: the kingdoms were cursed never to be mapped |
| Ender pearl, `/home`, or other teleports out | refused: *"The halls do not let go."* |

The same applies inside **the Mouth** on the surface.

**Why:** the layout is the layout. Nobody tunnels past a barred gate, bridges over a hazard, pillars
up to a ledge, or digs into the treasury from behind. Every route the generator guarantees stays true
for the whole delve.

**The dead obey the seal too.** Endermen and silverfish can't move blocks. Ravagers don't trample
anything. Nothing below griefs.

### Nothing breaks

Your gear takes **no durability damage** below. No repair bills, no mending sessions after. The cost
of a fight is health, potions and the bag, never your sword.

### Heirlooms never break, anywhere

**Heirlooms**, the named gear found in treasuries and on kings, are **unbreakable** above and below.
The anvil, the grindstone and the smithing table **refuse them**: the surface can't mend them, change
them or make them greater.

### Working a vein

Ore is the one exception to "nothing breaks." Break an ore block and:

- you get the **kingdom's own treasure** for that ore, not vanilla ore (see
  [Hoards, Veins and Loot](Hoards-Veins-and-Loot.md))
- the block **turns into the surrounding rock**. The vein is spent and can't be worked twice.
- **Fortune** gives you more. **Silk Touch** is refused.
- If your pick is too weak for the ore, the first swing rings off the rock: *"Too soft a pick for so
  hard a vein. Bring an iron pick."*

Mining takes seconds you spend standing still under the Waking. Choose your veins.

### Stuck?

With no blocks to place, a one-way drop can trap you. Type **`/delve unstick`** to return to the
door of the room you're in. It has a 60-second cooldown and skips nothing, because the door is where
you came from.

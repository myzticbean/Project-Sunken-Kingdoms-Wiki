# Hoards, Veins and Loot

There are two sources of loot below: **hoards** (containers placed in the halls) and **veins** (ore
in the walls). Common dead drop nothing. Wights and kings always do.

---

## Containers

| Container | How common | Where | Watched? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decorated pot** | most | anywhere you can reach | no. Smash it for minor loot |
| **Barrel** | common | on the floor | no. Open freely |
| **Chest (hoard)** | standard | about a third on the **highest reachable ledge**, the rest on the floor, never under water | **yes** |
| **Gilded chest** | one per hold | the **treasury**, the hold's deepest cave room | **yes** |

Hoard chests are **old copper**, aged anywhere from fresh to green, and they stay the age you found
them at. The gilded chest is the only bright one in the hold. There are no plain wooden chests below.

A high-ledge chest always has a way up. It's never bait.

## Watched hoards and robbing

While any of a room's dead still stand, its hoards are **watched**: **soul particles smoke from them**.

### Option 1: Clear the room first

Put down every dead anchored to that room. You hear a chime and see **"The hall is quiet."** The
hoards stop smoking and open like any chest. *Quiet is per room*: a chase that spills into the next
hall doesn't affect it.

### Option 2: Rob it

Click a smoking hoard while its keepers still stand:

1. **It doesn't open.** The click *is* the robbing.
2. **Keepers rise** around it, out of the floor within 8 blocks: 2 + the current band. From band 3 up,
   they're always marked.
3. A curse sounds and the action bar reads **"The dead stir."**
4. The **Waking jumps 5 minutes** (a third of a band).
5. The hoard is **locked**. Click it while locked and its keepers **glow** for three seconds in the
   hoard's own colour, so you know which ones to chase.
6. When the last keeper is down (or has been lured so far it sinks), the shutter sound plays and the
   **lid swings open**. The loot is exactly what it would have been.

A hoard is never robbed twice, and it stops smoking once robbed.

**Robbing gives no bonus loot.** It trades *clock minutes* for *real minutes*. That's a bad trade
unless you're short of real minutes, so robbing is the tool of the player with fifteen minutes to
spare. That's the point.

### Emptied hoards crumble

Empty a hoard, close it, and it **crumbles to dust**. The halls let it go. There's never an empty
chest to put things back into.

## What's guaranteed

Most loot is rolled, but some things are promised, because a delve with none of them is just a bad
night:

| Where | Guaranteed |
|---|---|
| Hold I (any tier) | at least **two healing potions** across its hoards |
| The gilded chest | one **heirloom** or one high-level enchanted piece |
| Every chest | one **enchanted piece** (likelier deeper), or one prize: a golden apple, a strong healing or splash potion, strength, regeneration, swiftness, or rarely a **totem** |
| Every hold | some **fire goods** (nether wart, blaze rods, glowstone, magma cream), so you can brew above in a world without a Nether |
| The treasury | one high-value roll |
| A wight | one kingdom treasure |
| A king | a **Seal** for every delver present, and one **heirloom** |

### What makes loot better

**Loot quality depends on how deep you are**: which hold, and how deep that room sits in the hold.
It **never** depends on the Waking. Waiting around never improves loot, so there's no reason to idle.

Party size adds one extra roll to every hoard per extra member, up to four.

## Stars

Every item that comes from below has **gold stars** (✦) after its name:

| Stars | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✦ | common: bulk |
| ✦✦ | mid: goods, potions |
| ✦✦✦ | rare: enchanted gear, heirlooms, relics |

A glance at the tooltip tells you what something is worth before you read a word of lore. Things
from below stack to **99**.

## Kingdom veins

The ore in the walls *looks* like coal, copper, iron, redstone, lapis, diamond and emerald. **It never
gives you vanilla ore.** Each kingdom's rock bears its own things, and every vein gives up what that
kingdom made of it.

> *Each kingdom's rock bears its own metal, and the kingdoms' gold was never the surface's gold.*

The same ore in two kingdoms gives two different things. Two ores that look different never give the
same thing. Each item has two lore lines: one of its own, then the kingdom's name and what it's for.

| Vein | The Greenwood | The Stonemark | The Hush |
|---|---|---|---|
| coal (common) | Rootchar | Forgeslag | Silence Ink |
| copper (common) | Rootcopper | Markbrass | Verdigris |
| iron (mid) | Ironbark Sap | Blastgrit | Hushwing |
| redstone (mid) | Rootwart | Hollowdust | Hushed Eye |
| lapis (mid) | Bluecap Lapis | Lapis of the Mark | Lapis of the Silent Court |
| diamond (rare) | Rooted King's Heart | Black Steel | Echo of the Silent Court |
| emerald (rare) | Sherd of the Harvest | Sherd of the Trial | Sherd of the Mourners |

**The Ashen Hold** has nether rock and three veins: quartz gives **Ashglass**, gold gives
**Cairnfire**, ancient debris gives **the Ashen King's Ember**.

Where cave vines hang, their **glow berries** are a common vein too, picked rather than mined: the
Greenwood's **Rootlight Berry**, the Stonemark's **Lampberry of the Mark**.

### What each tier is for

- **Common** is for the **Assayer**. It gets struck into Crowns. It does nothing else.
- **Mid** is **workshop stock**. Iron and redstone veins give **brewing ingredients**, lapis gives
  **enchanting lapis**. Fully usable above. This is how brewing works in a world with no Nether.
- **Rare** is a **relic**: high Crown value, an enchantment glint, a trophy for an item frame. The
  first one of its kind carried up **brings a new factor to town**. No factor ever sells one.

The deeper the kingdom, the more its things are worth. Rare veins appear more in deeper holds.

## Heirlooms

**Heirlooms** are named pieces of gear with special attribute bonuses and enchantments beyond the
normal limits. They come from gilded chests, kings, and (planned) the Tithe.

- **Unbreakable**, above and below.
- **Refused** by the anvil, the grindstone and the smithing table.
- **Never for sale.** No factor will buy or sell one.

They're the build. There's no skill tree; the gear you find is how your character grows.

## Tagged items: the kingdom's things

Everything you **find** below is **tagged** to that delve. It belongs to the kingdom until you walk
it out.

| Where | What you can do with tagged loot |
|---|---|
| Below | carry it, or pass it hand to hand to any member of your delve |
| Below | **not** put it in any chest, barrel, hopper or item frame. The dead would only take it back |
| Walk out | the tag is removed. It's an ordinary item forever: chest it, trade it, frame it, brew with it |
| You die or quit | it stays below as your **remains** |
| Dawn | **lost**, for everyone |

**What you brought down is always yours.** Only found loot is at stake.

A friend can carry your bag out for you. That costs your party a member (walking out is final), which
is the price.

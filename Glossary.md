# Glossary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Anchor** | the room a dead belongs to. It won't chase you far from it |
| **Arrival arch** | the arch you land beside after crossing a Waygate. Never barred, always two-way, and you can start a Holdout at it |
| **Assayer, the** | the factor who buys what you bring up, for Crowns, by right-click. Also changes coin |
| **Band** | one step of the Waking, 0 to 5: Sleeping, Stirring, Waking, Risen, Marching, the Host |
| **Bellmouth** | the town at the Mouth of the Cairn. Built by players around the hearth |
| **Breath, the** | the delve's health rule: +10 hearts on arrival, no natural healing, the dead heal you when put down |
| **Cairn, the** | the mountain heaped over the sunken kingdoms. Spawn is on its side |
| **Call, the** | a Hush shrieker's cry: darkness, and every Hush dead in reach comes for whoever set it off |
| **Chronicle, the** | *(planned)* an advancement tab of delve achievements |
| **Companies, the** | the crews who came to strip each kingdom as it failed and sank with it. Their timber props, scaffolds and lanterns fill the upper holds |
| **Crown** | the town's coin: a glinting gold nugget. A **Crown Bar** is 100 |
| **Dawn** | the end of a delve, 90 minutes after the first fall. Everything still tagged is lost |
| **Dead, the** | everything hostile below. Nothing below is alive, so the dead are only ever put down |
| **Delve** | one run, from the knock to the last delver out or dawn |
| **Drop, the** | how you enter every hold: falling from above, with Slow Falling |
| **Factor** | a trader at the hearth. Factors arrive as new treasures come up |
| **Gatehouse** | the one built room in each hold, holding the barred Waygate onward |
| **Gilded chest** | the bright copper chest in each hold's treasury. Holds an heirloom or a high enchant |
| **Hearth, the** | spawn: a fire under the Upper Half, the wall and the factors |
| **Heirloom** | named gear with special bonuses, and a gift of the kingdom it came from: blessed, plain or flawed, by luck (see [Patch Notes](Patch-Notes.md)). Unbreakable. No anvil, grindstone or smithing table will take it. Never for sale |
| **Hoard** | a chest placed below. Barrels and pots are not hoards |
| **Hold** | one sunken kingdom: one generated cave network, one stage of a delve |
| **Holdout** | breaking a gate homeward: strike its bell and hold the threshold while waves rise |
| **Homecoming** | the bright ring of the Upper Half when someone walks out |
| **Household** | the dead a king calls to his side at two-thirds and one-third health |
| **Keeper** | a dead raised by robbing a hoard. The hoard is locked until its keepers are down |
| **King** | the ruler waiting in the Throne at the end of a delve. One per kingdom, named fresh each delve |
| **King's Bell** | the bell over the king's stand. It tolls itself when he falls and opens the way home |
| **Kit, the** | free starting gear from the Provisioner, once per player |
| **Knell, the** | the low ring of the Upper Half when someone falls below |
| **Knock, the** | the bell's answer from below when you strike a ring: tolls climbing up the shaft until the cap falls |
| **Listening post** | a dark side-pocket in the Hush where the shriekers are. Never on the route |
| **Mark** | one power on a dead: Fleet, Ironbound, Unresting, Wrathful or Shadowed, shown by colour |
| **Membership** | the list of players in a delve. You join by falling in, and leave only by walking out |
| **Mouth, the** | the hall cut into the Cairn holding the rings |
| **Oathbound, the** | the kings' sworn knights who answer a Holdout's bell: the Knight (iron golem) and the King's Hound (ravager) |
| **Peal, the** | the tolling of a Holdout, one toll per wave |
| **Processional** | the dressed tunnel into the Throne |
| **Provisioner, the** | the factor who gives the kit and sells food and supplies |
| **Purse** | a gold bundle that holds any number of Crowns in one slot |
| **Put down** | what you do to the dead. Nothing below is alive, so nothing below dies |
| **Quiet** | a room whose dead are all down. Its hoards open freely: *"The hall is quiet."* |
| **Remains** | your head, bones and red name where you fell or quit, holding your found loot until dawn |
| **Ring** | a well in the Mouth with a bell in an arch above it. One delve at a time |
| **Robbing** | opening a watched hoard before its room is quiet. Raises keepers and advances the Waking |
| **Seal** | the tier unlock, pressed from a king's signet. One for every delver present. Never consumed |
| **Seal, the (rule)** | the halls are held: no placing, no breaking but ore, no durability loss, no regeneration |
| **Strata** | the layers of rock in a hold. The deeper you go, the darker the stone |
| **Sunken Bell, the** | the Upper Half, the ring bells and every arch bell, treated as one bell |
| **Tagged** | found below, still belonging to the kingdom. Walking out removes the tag |
| **Threshold** | the floor under an arch's bell. The centre of a Holdout |
| **Throne, the** | the king's crypt at the end of the last hold |
| **Tier** | how deep a delve goes: the Undercroft, the Deep Halls, the Ashen Reach |
| **Tithe, the** | *(planned)* three offerings beside every arrival arch. Take one |
| **Treasury** | each hold's deepest cave room, with the gilded chest |
| **Upper Half, the** | the top half of the great bell, hung over the hearth. It mirrors every bell below |
| **Vein** | ore in the walls. Gives the kingdom's own treasure, works once, then turns to rock |
| **Vow** | *(planned)* an optional modifier sworn at the ring before a delve |
| **Waking, the** | the difficulty clock: time, robbing and crossing gates make the dead stronger |
| **Wall, the** | the inscriptions and records at the hearth |
| **Watched** | a hoard whose room still has dead standing. It smokes with soul particles |
| **Waygate** | the arch between two holds. Barred until its objective is won |
| **Wight** | a glowing, named, marked elite dead with a guaranteed treasure drop |

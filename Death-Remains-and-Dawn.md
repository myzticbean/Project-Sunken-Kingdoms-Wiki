# Death, Remains and Dawn

> *A delver who falls is left where they fell, and the halls hold that as they hold everything else.*

**Keep what you brought. Go back for what you found.**

## What happens when...

| Event | Your found (tagged) loot | You | The delve |
|---|---|---|---|
| **You walk out** through a homeward gate | **untagged, yours forever** | set down in your ring, **off the list** | continues for everyone else |
| **You die** | left as **remains** where you fell | wake at the hearth, **still on the list** | continues |
| **You quit** mid-delve | left as **remains** where you stood | back at your ring when you rejoin, still on the list | continues |
| **Dawn** (90 minutes after the first fall) | **lost**, for everyone, remains included | set down at your ring | **ends** |
| **The server shuts down** | **untagged**, yours (deliberately generous) | at your ring | ends |

**Everything you brought down is always kept.** You never drop your own gear: inventory is kept on
death below. Only the kingdom's things are at stake.

## Remains

When you die or quit below, you leave **remains** at the spot:

- **your own head** on the ground, facing the way you faced
- **bones** strewn around it
- **your name in red** floating over it
- soul flames drifting around it

The name and the outline of the bones **show through walls** until someone from your delve is close.
Up close, it's just a pile.

**Right-click the head** to open the **bag**: every tagged item you were carrying. Take the last
item and the head, bones and name vanish with a crack of bone.

### Rules

- **One set of remains per player.** Die again and your remains **move** to the new spot; the old
  head is gone. You never have two piles.
- While you're **online and still in the delve**, only **you** can open your bag.
- Once you've **quit or walked out**, **anyone** in the delve can open it and carry your things out
  for you.
- Remains last **until dawn**, or until emptied.

## Going back down

Death doesn't end your night. The ring **still answers you**:

1. You wake at the hearth.
2. Walk back into your ring's open shaft and fall in. You land at Hold I.
3. Descend through the Waygates your party already opened.
4. Find your bones, open the bag.

But the **Waking didn't wait for you**. Your bag is probably four rooms and a band deeper than when
you lost it, the way back is longer than the way there was, and going for it may cost you the delve.

That's the point: a wipe becomes a **decision**, not a deletion.

## Dawn

> *A gate holds for one night. At dawn the way closes.*

A delve can last at most **90 minutes from the first fall**.

- Warnings at **10 minutes** and **2 minutes**: a title and low thunder from above. (Dawn is the one
  warning that's not a bell.)
- At dawn, **everything tagged is lost**, remains included, and everyone still below is set down at
  their ring.

The 90-minute ceiling is there to stop a delve becoming a base, not to hurry you. Almost no delve
needs that long.

## No insurance, no bank

There's no way to stash loot below or insure it. **Breaking a gate homeward already is partial
extraction**, at a price that grows with depth. A bank on top would remove the only stake the delve
has.

## Optional harsher rules (server setting)

- `death.remains: false`: dying **deletes** your found loot outright, no remains.
- `death.hard-mode`: dying also **destroys the gear you brought down**. Off by default.

# The Kingdoms

Four sunken kingdoms lie under the Cairn, and two more are rumoured. Each **hold** in a delve is one
kingdom: its own rock, its own dead, its own hazard, its own ore, and its own king.

Within one hold the rock is one rock. Between holds there is only dark, bridged by Waygates. So a
single delve can take you from a drowned forest straight into a silent court.

| Kingdom | Look | King | First met in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Greenwood** | lush caves, roots, moss, pools | the Rooted King | Tier 1, the Undercroft |
| **The Stonemark** | grey stone, masonry, dripstone | the Hollow King | Tier 1, the Undercroft |
| **The Hush** | deep dark, sculk | the Silent King | Tier 2, the Deep Halls |
| **The Ashen Hold** | nether rock, magma, lava | the Ashen King | Tier 3, the Ashen Reach |
| The Winter Court | frozen | — | *planned* |
| The Glass Court | crystal | — | *planned* |

The deeper the tier, the more kingdoms are in the pool. The kingdoms for each delve are drawn at
random, and no kingdom appears twice in one descent while the pool lasts. The **last** hold's kingdom
decides whose Throne you face.

## The Greenwood

> *A forest kingdom drowned in root and water.*

The Greenwood went down with its trees. Its halls are caverns of moss and hanging roots, still pools
and glow berries, with old brickwork half swallowed by green. It is the gentlest-looking kingdom and
often the first one you see.

**Hazard: thorn and web.** Sweet berry bushes grow in the flora and cobwebs gather in low corners.
Thorns cut you and webs hold you in place while the dead close in. None of it is ever on the shortest
path between doors. You only step into it if you leave the path.

**The dead of the Greenwood**

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| Rootbound | zombie |
| Poolshade | drowned, favouring the pools |
| Thornspinner | spider |
| Rootbiter | cave spider |
| **Rooted Thane** (wight) | armoured zombie |

**The king: the Rooted King.** A towering zombie (1.6× size) crowned in gold, in chainmail, carrying
an axe. His banners, candles and honour guard are green. His name is drawn each delve from an old
pool: *Cenwulf, Eadric, Wulfhere, Aldhelm.*

## The Stonemark

> *The masons' kingdom.*

The Stonemark built in stone and was buried in it. Grey and tuff-banded caverns, terraces cut like
quarry steps, and more surviving masonry than anywhere else. Its gatehouses and its Throne are the
work of people who were proud of their walls.

**Hazard: spikes.** Pointed dripstone stands on terrace edges and in the floors of pools. Fall off a
ledge onto it, or wade into the wrong pool, and you will feel it.

**The dead of the Stonemark**

| Hollow Mason | skeleton |
| Wallward | zombie in iron |
| Stonemite | silverfish (rare) |
| **Hollow Warden** (wight) | armoured skeleton |

**The king: the Hollow King.** A towering zombie in iron. Names: *Osric, Ceolwulf, Beornwulf, Ine.*

## The Hush

> *A court that swore silence, and was eaten by it.*

The Hush is the **stealth kingdom**. Its halls are dark and grown over with sculk. Its dead make no
sound until they have found you, and they barely see or hear anything anymore. **But the sculk still
listens for them.**

**Hazard and rule: the Listening.** This is the Hush's whole personality:

- **The dead are deaf.** A Hush dead only notices you when you're close (about 8 blocks). Until it
  does, it is silent. Once it finds you, it finds its voice: a groan as it turns, and every sound after
  that.
- **The sculk is not deaf.** A sculk sensor hears your footsteps, a thrown snowball, anything, even
  through rock. When it hears you, the dead nearby **walk over to look**.
- **The Call.** Shriekers live in **listening posts**, dark side-pockets placed well off every route
  between doors. Walk the route and nothing cries out. Step into a listening post carelessly and the
  shrieker **screams**: the room goes dark for a breath, and every Hush dead within reach, standing or
  asleep, wakes up, **glows through the rock**, and comes straight for whoever set it off. A
  shrieker's first cry also pushes the Waking clock forward, and from the Risen band on it raises fresh
  dead from the floor.
- **Sneaking is the counterplay.** It's vanilla sneaking. Nothing new to learn.

You can play the Hush quietly: read the sculk like radar, throw things to lure the dead away, rob
hoards right under their noses, and reach the king before he knows you're there. Or you can play it
loud and fight everything at once.

**The dead of the Hush**

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| Hushbone | skeleton |
| Mute | zombie |
| Listener | enderman |
| **Silent Herald** (wight) | wither skeleton |

**The king: the Silent King.** A **warden**, and the only warden in the game. He sits under his bell
in the Hush's Throne, and he **remembers**: he goes first for the delver who made the sculk cry most.
A party that never set off a shrieker finds him rising calm. Names: *Aethelred, Edwy, Sigeberht.*

## The Ashen Hold

> *A kingdom burned down into the underworld.*

The oldest and deepest kingdom. Its rock is nether rock: netherrack, basalt and blackstone, magma
floors and lava pockets. This is where **the only fire left in the world** burns.

**Hazard: fire.** Magma on the floors near lava, and lava pockets carved below floor level. At tier
3, lava pools crossed on stepping stones are part of the terrain.

**The dead of the Ashen Hold**

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| Cinderbone | wither skeleton |
| Emberwisp | blaze |
| Slagborn | magma cube |
| Ashboar | hoglin |
| **Ashen Housecarl** (wight) | piglin brute |

**The king: the Ashen King.** A **wither**. Names: *Penda, Caedwalla, Offa.*

**Veins of the Ashen Hold**

| Looks like | Gives | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| quartz | Ashglass | common |
| gold | **Cairnfire** | mid |
| ancient debris | **the Ashen King's Ember** | rare |

The first Cairnfire carried up brings **the Alchemist** to Bellmouth: fire for a brewing stand, in a
world with no Nether.

## The rumoured courts *(planned)*

- **The Winter Court**: frozen halls. Cold spreads across a join the way sculk does, so pools near it
  ice over.
- **The Glass Court**: crystal halls.

Neither has a king yet.

---

## When kingdoms meet *(planned, tier 4+)*

In the deepest tier, **the Sundered Realms**, kingdoms mix inside one hold:

- **Greenwood and Stonemark**: two provinces of one world. The tunnel between them thins out its moss
  and gives way to gravel.
- **Any kingdom and the Hush**: sculk creeps out of the Hush into the near side of the tunnel.
- **Anything and the Ashen Hold**: never a tunnel. Burnt rock and living rock share no geology, so the
  two are joined only by a Waygate somebody built.

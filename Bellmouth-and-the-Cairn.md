# Bellmouth and the Cairn

The surface is where you prepare, trade, build and rest. The delve is where everything else happens.

## The valley

The world above is an ordinary Minecraft overworld inside a **1,000-block world border**. You can
cut timber, mine stone and iron and diamonds, farm, cook, brew and build, the way you always have.

What is gone is the **Nether and the End**. In the lore, the only fire left in the world burns under
the Cairn. In practice, the delve replaces the vanilla "beat the dragon" arc: the tiers below *are*
the progression.

## The Cairn

The Cairn is the mountain at the heart of the valley: the heap the earth raised over the sunken
kingdoms. The server finds a mountain peak near the world's centre and sets spawn on its side.
Nobody digs into the Cairn. The only way in is the Mouth.

## The Mouth

The **Mouth** is a cave hall cut into the Cairn's flank. It is the front door to the delve. On its
floor are the **rings**:

- Each ring is a **well**: a grated shaft with a cap of stone over it, under an arch.
- A **bell** hangs in the arch above each ring. Every one of these bells was carried up out of the
  Cairn by a founder, and each still answers the gate it was cut from.
- Beside each ring are two unlit fires and a plinth.
- Floating text over each bell shows **which tier** the ring is set to.

The Mouth is held like the halls below: you can't break or build anything inside it.

**One delve per ring.** While a party is below, its ring is taken. Strike it and you get a single
dull toll and the message *"The bell is already answered."* Use another ring.

## The hearth

You spawn at **the hearth**, at the entrance of the Mouth:

- **A fire**, with **the Upper Half** hung over it: the top half of the great bell, the first thing
  the heirs carried out.
- **The wall**: floating inscriptions that tell you what to do in three couplets, plus the **records**
  (deepest hold, fastest Throne for each tier, highest band anyone walked out at) and the latest
  extractions by name.
- **The factors**: villagers who trade with delvers. See [Crowns and Factors](Crowns-and-Factors).

When you first join you get a short book, *The Wall*, with the same three couplets:

> *Strike the bell above a ring,*
> *and fall to the halls of a sunken king.*
> *Below, the arch bell tolls you down;*
> *a broken gate tolls you home to town.*
> *What you bear up is yours alone;*
> *what you fall with waits beside your bones.*

## The Upper Half

The Upper Half is one bell with every bell below. Anything that happens down there, it hears:

| The Upper Half... | When |
|---|---|
| rings once, bright and high | someone walks out through a homeward gate (**homecoming**) |
| rings once, low, unbidden | someone falls below (**the knell**) |
| swings with every toll | someone is fighting their way out through a Holdout (**the peal**) |

So anyone sitting at the hearth can follow the night below just by listening.

## The town itself

Everything else in Bellmouth is **built by the players**: the tavern, walls, roofs, beds, the bar,
the road. The plugin places only the anchors: the Mouth, the rings, the hearth and its factors. By
custom the first house at the Mouth is the tavern.

Beds set your spawn as usual. Rest is part of the story, not a mechanic.

## Growing the town

Factors **arrive** when something new comes up from below. The first time any player on the server
carries up a certain kingdom treasure, the factor who deals in it comes to Bellmouth for good. A new
player joining later finds a richer town than the founders had, and the town visibly grows because of
what people did below.

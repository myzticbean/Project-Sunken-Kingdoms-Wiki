# Ways to Play

**There is no minimum session.** Fifteen minutes is a real delve. There's no early-exit penalty of any
kind. The only cost of leaving early is what you didn't go and get.

## Five kinds of night

| Time | Route | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **10–15 min** | Clear Hold I, walk back, out through the free gate | everything you found, at full health |
| **10–15 min, greedy** | Rob every hoard in Hold I, out through the free gate before the band matters | the same loot, faster, more fights, fewer hearts |
| **~20 min, greedier** | Rob Hold I, then strike the gatehouse bell at band 1 and hold out 45 seconds | the same loot, plus a climax |
| **25–45 min** | Tier 1 to the Throne: two small holds, put the king down, press the Seal | everything, plus the next tier |
| **60–90 min** | Tier 2 or 3, holds swept, out through a Holdout at band 4 | everything |

## Session to session: why one more

- **The descent is new every time**: kingdom order, layouts, wight placement, marks, events.
- **The near miss.** You saw the Throne's processional and turned back at band 4.
- **The hoard you robbed at band 4 and got away with**, and the one you didn't.
- **The heirloom that rolled one bonus short** of the one you wanted.
- **The tier you just unlocked** and haven't seen.
- **The factor who came to town** because of what you brought up last night.
- **Your line on the wall**, and whoever beat it.
- **The friend who's on tomorrow**, for a Throne that's genuinely easier with three.

**Deliberately absent:** daily quests, login rewards, expiring currencies, streaks. Missing a day
costs nothing.

## Tactics

### Health is your clock
- **Fight to heal.** Every common dead is a heart. In a long delve, skipping fights is how you run dry.
- **Save potions for spikes**: the gatehouse, a Holdout, the Throne. Not for chip damage.
- The **final blow** gets the Breath. In a party, let whoever's lowest finish things off.

### Rob or clear?
- **Short on real time?** Rob. You trade clock-minutes for real minutes.
- **Going deep?** Clear. Robbing early makes every later hold harder (+5 minutes each).
- **A trick:** rob, then **kite the keepers** out past the room's leash. They sink, and the hoard
  unlocks. It costs the walk.

### Read the bell
- **The hum** means a band change in 30 seconds. Finish what you're doing.
- **On every Waking toll, look around.** Every dead within 32 blocks glows for five seconds. That's
  your free scout.
- **Shadowed** (invisible) dead show their outline on every toll.

### Route smart
- Wights **glow through rock**. Route around them if you're low.
- Hazards are **never on the shortest door-to-door path**. If you're standing in thorns or on
  dripstone, you left the path.
- **Look through the gatehouse arch** before you commit. Its frame shows the next kingdom's rock.

### The Hush
- **Sneak.** The dead there notice you only close.
- **Sculk sensors** draw the dead to look. Throw a snowball at one across the room to pull them away.
- **Stay off the listening posts.** They're never on the route. If it's dark and grown over and off
  to the side, back out.
- If you never set off a shrieker, **the Silent King rises calm**.

### Holdouts
- **Strike early in the band.** The charge is fixed at the band you struck at, so strike before the
  next toll.
- **Fight at the arch.** Leaving the 12-block circle pauses the charge but nothing else.
- Waves are healing. Don't flee them.

### Death
- Your remains **glow through walls**. You'll find them.
- If a friend walks out, they **can't come back**. If they die, they can.
- Once you've walked out, a friend still below can open your remains and carry your bag out.

### Preparation above
- **Brew.** Fire goods (nether wart, blaze rods, glowstone, magma cream) come in every hold's hoards.
  Mid-tier vein treasures are brewing ingredients.
- **Cook.** Food doesn't heal below, but sprinting needs it.
- **Mine the valley** for diamonds and gold. The tier 2 target is enchanted diamond and golden apples.

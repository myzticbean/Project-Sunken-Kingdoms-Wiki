# The Bell

> *They are one bell: strike any and the others answer.*

The **Upper Half** over the hearth, the **bells over the rings** in the Mouth, and the **bell in
every Waygate arch** below are, in the story, **one instrument**: the Sunken Bell. Anything that
happens at one is heard at the others.

That single idea is the whole sound design. Every important moment in the game has a voice, and
there's barely any HUD.

## The grammar

A bell says three things and nothing else:

| | Means |
|---|---|
| **Count** | information: how many tiers deep, or which band of the Waking |
| **Pitch** | depth: the lower the bell, the deeper or worse things are |
| **Silence** | suspense: the pause before an answer |

**No other system in the game plays a bell sound, ever.** So a toll is never ambiguous: if you hear a
bell, it means something.

## What every toll means

| You hear | Where | It means |
|---|---|---|
| **The knock**: your strike, two seconds of silence, then tolls climbing up the shaft, each one lower, ending in thunder | at a ring | the ground is opening. **3 tolls = tier 1, 5 = tier 2, 7 = tier 3** |
| **One dull, quiet toll** | at a ring | **refused**: the ring is busy, or you've already walked out of this delve, or you don't hold the Seal for that tier |
| **A faint hum** | below, close by | the Waking will climb in 30 seconds |
| **N tolls, a second apart** | below, at every delver | the Waking just reached band N. **Every dead nearby glows** |
| **Cracked double tolls** | below | **band 5, the Host** |
| **The peal**: the arch bell swinging by itself, once per wave, each toll lower | below, the whole hold | someone has struck a gate's bell and is fighting their way out (**Holdout**) |
| **A deep resonance as the gate flips** | below | the Holdout is done. The way home is open |
| **A hum every two seconds, leading you on** | the last hold | **the Throne** is near. It stops at the hall's door |
| **One deep toll, five seconds after the king falls** | the Throne | the **King's Bell** has tolled itself. The way home is open on his stand |
| **One bright, high ring** | the Upper Half, at the hearth | **homecoming**: someone walked out |
| **One low ring, unbidden** | the Upper Half | **the knell**: someone fell below |
| **The Upper Half swinging, again and again** | the hearth | someone below is in a Holdout right now |

## Dawn is not a bell

Dawn, the 90-minute end of a delve, is the one warning that comes **from above**: a title and a
roll of low thunder. Not a bell.

## Listening from the tavern

Because the Upper Half mirrors everything below, a player sitting at the hearth can follow the
night: a peal means someone is fighting their way out, a bright ring means they made it, a low ring
means someone didn't.

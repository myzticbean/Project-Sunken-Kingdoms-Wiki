# Getting Out

> *You can leave through any Waygate at any moment, but the dead keep what is theirs.*

Walking out is how found loot becomes yours. There are three ways.

| Route | Where | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **The free gate** | Hold I's entrance arch | nothing. Walk there |
| **The Holdout** | any other arch | a fight: hold the threshold while the waves come |
| **The King's Bell** | the Throne, after the king is put down | nothing more. The fight was the price |

Whichever way you go, passing through a homeward gate:

- **removes the tag** from everything you carry. It's yours, permanently
- sets you down **standing in your ring** in the Mouth
- rings the Upper Half once, bright: **homecoming**
- takes you **off the delve's list**. It's final for tonight: the ring won't let you back into this
  delve. Your friends below carry on

---

## The free gate

The arch you fell in through, at Hold I's entrance, is **bound to your bell**. It always points home
and costs nothing. Every Waygate you've crossed stays open both ways, so you can always **walk back
up** through the holds to reach it.

It's free, safe and slow. The clock keeps running while you walk, and rooms you cleared refill behind
you.

## The Holdout: breaking a gate homeward

> *Carrying a hoard up through a Waygate means striking its bell to call the gate, and the whole hold
> hears.*

**Strike the bell in any arch** (a gatehouse's barred gate, or an arrival arch you came through) and
the **peal** begins.

1. A title appears: **Hold the Way Home**, with the seconds you must stand.
2. A **charge bar** starts, fixed at the band you struck at.
3. **Stay within 12 blocks of the threshold.** Step out and the charge pauses. Nothing else pauses:
   the Waking and the night run on.
4. **Every 10 seconds the bell tolls and a wave rises** out of the floor around you, 6 to 12 blocks
   from the arch.
5. On the last toll, the gate **flips**: **The Way Home Is Open**. The doorway fills with **blue**
   (onward gates are black, so you can never confuse them).
6. You have **15 seconds** to step through.

| Band | Charge | Wave size (solo) | Oathbound (if the tier allows) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 30 s | 2 | none |
| 1 | 45 s | 3 | none |
| 2 | 60 s | 4 | none |
| 3 | 75 s | 5 | Knight (tier 2+) |
| 4 | 90 s | 6 | Knight and Hound (tier 3+) |
| 5 | 105 s | 7 | the Host: two Knights and a Hound |

Waves get bigger with party size. **Tier 1 never sends Oathbound.**

**Waves are healing.** Every dead you put down returns Breath, so a long Holdout is survivable if you
fight. Running away from the arch is a mistake twice over: the charge pauses, and you stop healing.

### What striking the bell gives up

Striking is **a choice the whole hold hears, and it can't be taken back**:

- the gate objective is dropped
- a gate already opened onward is **barred again**. This hold gives up the way deeper for good
- at a gatehouse, the bars part for the window, **toward home only**, then close

**If nobody makes it through** the window, you can strike the bell again. You lose nothing but the
waves already fought.

### Why a Holdout instead of walking back?

A walk back is dead time that punishes depth twice: once for the distance, and again because rooms
refill. The Holdout makes leaving **its own climax**, and it prices depth honestly: the deeper you
are and the more the hold has woken, the more it costs to cut your way out.

## The King's Bell

> *A king's own bell tolls itself for his death, and on his stand before the throne the way home
> opens and stays open: nothing below is left to hold it shut.*

The **King's Bell** hangs on a chain over the king's stand in the Throne.

- **Strike it while he stands** and it refuses.
- **Five seconds after he falls**, it **tolls itself**, once and deep, and **the way home opens on his
  stand** before the throne.
- **No Holdout.** It stays open until the delve ends.

## The push-or-bank decision

Risk spikes in exactly three places: **the gatehouse**, **breaking a gate homeward**, and **the
Throne**. Everything else is free exploration.

At every gatehouse, you face the real choice:

- **Break the gate homeward now.** Strike the bell, hold the peal, keep everything.
- **Cross.** +10 minutes on the clock. A whole new kingdom of hoards beyond. The Throne past that.
- **Walk back up.** Free and safe, but slow, and the clock keeps running.

Everything you need to decide is in front of you: the far kingdom's rock in the arch, your bag in
your inventory, your hearts on screen, the band on the bar.

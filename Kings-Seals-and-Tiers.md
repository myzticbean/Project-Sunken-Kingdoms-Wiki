# Kings, Seals and Tiers

> *An evening buys the Throne, the king's crypt, and his Seal pressed from the signet on his hand,
> which opens the next depth.*

## The tiers

The deeper tiers are your progression. Each one is a longer descent with older kingdoms, and each is
opened by the Seal of a king from the tier above.

| Tier | Name | Holds | Kingdoms in the pool | Whose Throne | Oathbound in Holdouts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The Undercroft** | 2 small | Greenwood, Stonemark | the Rooted King or the Hollow King | never |
| **2** | **The Deep Halls** | 3 | + **the Hush** | **the Silent King** | Knight from band 3 |
| **3** | **The Ashen Reach** | 4 | + **the Ashen Hold** | **the Ashen King** | Knight and Hound from band 4 |
| 4+ | The Sundered Realms *(planned)* | 5+ | mixed-kingdom holds | the last hold's king | the Host at band 5 |

Each tier changes the game **in kind**, not just in numbers:

- **Tier 1 teaches the loop.** Two small holds, one sitting of 25–45 minutes to your first Throne.
- **Tier 2 takes information away.** Darkness, sculk, deaf dead, and a king who hunts by sound.
- **Tier 3 makes the terrain hostile.** Lava crossed on stepping stones, fire underfoot, and a wither
  on the throne.
- **Tier 4** *(planned)* **makes the world unstable.** Kingdoms mixed inside one hold, and mandatory
  curses for better spoils.

## Choosing your tier at a ring

- Floating text over each ring's bell shows its current tier.
- **Sneak and strike** the bell to cycle the tier, up to the highest you've unlocked.
- Strike a ring set above your Seals and it refuses: *"The deeper Waygates open only to one who bears
  the Seal of the king above."*
- Only **the striker** needs the unlock. **Anyone can fall in.** A tier 3 player can carry their
  friends down.

## What you'll want to be wearing

There's no Nether. The Cairn *is* the Nether now. The tier ladder doubles as the gear ladder:

| Tier | Gear target | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the kit | the Provisioner, once, free |
| 2 | enchanted diamond, brewed potions, golden apples | valley diamonds and gold; **fire goods from tier-1 hoards** to brew |
| 3 | netherite, a full potion kit | ancient debris from tier 2's deepest veins, common in the Ashen Hold |

Tier 1 is beatable in the kit with only the potions its own hoards give you.

## The Throne

The last hold has no gatehouse. Its deepest room is the **king's crypt**, a built hall:

1. **The processional.** A straight tunnel, paved, hung with soul lanterns on chains and banners in
   the kingdom's colour.
2. **The hum.** As you approach, the bell resonates every two seconds, **leading you toward the hall**.
3. **Silence.** At the door, the hum stops. That's your cue.
4. **The hall.** An aisle between two rows of banner-hung columns, an **honour guard** of armoured
   statues, candles, braziers, and at the far end a **tiered dais** with the throne on top and the
   **King's Bell** hanging over the king's stand.

### The king rises

When a delver comes near the dais, the **king rises** out of it: a toll, his **title** on screen,
and his own **boss bar**.

Every king has a **name drawn fresh each delve**, so the wall reads *put down Osric the Hollow King
at band 2*, not the same line every night.

| King | Kingdom | What he is | Possible names |
|---|---|---|---|
| **the Rooted King** | the Greenwood | a giant zombie (1.6×), gold-crowned, axe | Cenwulf, Eadric, Wulfhere, Aldhelm |
| **the Hollow King** | the Stonemark | a giant zombie in iron (1.6×) | Osric, Ceolwulf, Beornwulf, Ine |
| **the Silent King** | the Hush | a **warden** | Aethelred, Edwy, Sigeberht |
| **the Ashen King** | the Ashen Hold | a **wither** | Penda, Caedwalla, Offa |

### The household

At **two-thirds** and **one-third** of his health, the king calls his **household**: his kingdom's
wight and marked dead, rising from the floor. Never more than six of them stand at once.

### The arena is the hall

You can't drag the fight elsewhere. If the king spends **12 seconds outside the hall**, he **sinks**
into the floor, his household with him, and the fight resets. He **doesn't heal**. The next delver
who comes near the dais raises him again at the same health.

### The Silent King remembers

In the Hush, the Silent King **goes first for the delver who made the sculk cry the most**. A party
that never set off a shrieker finds him rising calm.

### When the king is put down

- His **crown stays where he fell**.
- **One heirloom** drops.
- **Every delver in the hall** receives a **Seal** straight into their hand: an impression of his
  signet, with the king's name in its lore.
- The **next tier unlocks** for each of them.
- Whoever landed the blow gets **all their Breath back**.
- One line in **server chat** announces it.
- **Five seconds later**, the **King's Bell tolls itself**, and the **way home opens on his stand**.
  No Holdout needed.

## Seals

> *A Seal is not taken; it is pressed from the signet still on the king's hand, and every delver in
> the crypt presses their own.*

- **One per delver present**, delivered to the hand. Nothing to fight over, nothing to carry for a
  friend who wasn't there.
- **Never consumed.** Your unlock is permanent and stored on your character.
- **Never for sale.** No factor will touch one.
- A trophy. Put it in an item frame.

## Why the king is back tomorrow

> *Nothing below is alive, so nothing below can die. A king is no different, and his crypt is the one
> room the halls never lose.*

He was only put down. When the next gate opens, he rises from where he lay, and
he's on his throne again.

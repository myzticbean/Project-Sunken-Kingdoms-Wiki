# Your First Delve

A walkthrough from your first login to your first trip home. A first delve into Hold I takes about
15 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Get the kit

You spawn at **the hearth**, under the Upper Half. Read the wall (or the book you were given), then
right-click **the Provisioner**.

The first time, the Provisioner gives you **the kit**, free and once only:

- iron helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots
- iron sword and iron pickaxe
- 16 bread
- 2 healing potions
- 32 torches

Nobody goes down the first time in their own boots. Tier 1 is balanced around this kit, so you don't
need anything else.

> **Tip:** Put on the armour. Keep the potions for emergencies. Healing is scarce below.

## Step 2: Find a free ring

Walk into **the Mouth**, the hall cut into the mountain behind the hearth. Each ring has a bell in an
arch above it, and floating text that shows its tier. On your first night only **tier 1, the
Undercroft** is open to you.

If the ring's bell gives a single dull toll and says *"The bell is already answered,"* someone is
already below through it. Pick another one.

## Step 3: Strike the bell (the knock)

Hit the bell. Then wait.

Two seconds of silence. Then **the answer comes from below**: a toll from deep in the shaft, then
another higher up and lower in pitch, each one climbing toward you. Soul fires light beside the ring.
The stone cap over the well cracks a block at a time and souls seep from the seams. On the last toll
the bell above you swings **on its own**, thunder rolls, and **the cap falls into the dark**.

The number of tolls tells you how deep you're going: **three for tier 1**, five for tier 2, seven for
tier 3.

> **Party tip:** Everyone falls through the **same ring**. The ring stays open for the whole delve, so
> friends who log on late can still fall in after you.

## Step 4: Fall in

Walk under the arch, down into the crater, through the gap in the parapet, and **step into the
shaft**.

You fall. Your vision goes black for a moment and the world swims. Then it clears and you are
**drifting slowly** down into a lit cavern, with Slow Falling holding you up. While you fall,
**your health bar grows**: ten extra hearts, thirty in all. That is **the Breath**.

A title names the kingdom you have fallen into: *The Greenwood*, or *The Stonemark*.

You have **5 minutes** after the knock to fall in. If nobody does, the ground closes again. The
night below, and **the Waking** clock, start only when the first delver lands.

## Step 5: Learn the rules of the halls

Within a minute you will notice that things work differently down here:

| You try to... | What happens |
|---|---|
| place a block | refused |
| break a block | refused, unless it is ore or a decorated pot |
| eat to heal | food only fuels sprinting. **Nothing heals on its own** |
| use your sword a lot | it never wears out. **Nothing breaks below** |
| open a map | it is blank |
| ender pearl or `/home` away | refused: *"The halls do not let go."* |

**How you heal:** put down the dead. Each one returns some Breath to whoever lands the final blow.
You'll see heart particles. See [The Breath and the Seal](The-Breath-and-the-Seal.md).

**If you get stuck** below a drop you can't climb back up, type `/delve unstick`. It returns you to the
door of the room you're in (60-second cooldown).

## Step 6: Explore the hold

A hold is a network of cave rooms joined by tunnels. **Nothing inside a hold is locked.** Go anywhere
in any order.

Things to look for:

- **The dead.** They rise out of the floor as you get near. Some carry a coloured **mark** that makes
  them faster, tougher or stronger. A **wight** glows through the rock, has a name, and hits hard.
  See [The Dead](The-Dead.md).
- **Hoards.** Old copper chests. If a hoard is **smoking with soul particles**, its room's dead still
  stand. Opening it now means **robbing** it. See below.
- **Barrels and pots.** Never watched. Open barrels and smash pots freely for minor loot.
- **Ore veins.** Look like coal, copper, iron and so on, but each gives up a **kingdom treasure**
  instead. Each vein can be worked once. See [Hoards, Veins and Loot](Hoards-Veins-and-Loot.md).

### Clearing versus robbing

When you put down the last of a room's dead, you hear a chime and see **"The hall is quiet."** The
hoards stop smoking and are free to open.

If you click a smoking hoard **before** that, you have robbed it:

- It **doesn't open**. Instead, more dead **rise around it**: its *keepers*.
- The hold wakes faster (the Waking jumps 5 minutes).
- The hoard is **locked** until every keeper is down. Then its lid swings open.

Robbing saves time but costs fights and clock. Clearing costs time and gives you health back. That
trade is the heart of the game.

## Step 7: Watch the bar, listen to the bell

At the top of the screen is a **six-segment bossbar**: the Waking. It starts at **Sleeping**. Every
15 minutes, and every time you rob or cross a gate, it climbs. Each time it moves up a band, **the
bell tolls** once per band, and every dead within 32 blocks **glows through the rock** for five
seconds.

Higher bands mean tougher, faster, more numerous dead. See [The Waking](The-Waking.md).

## Step 8: Decide how to leave

You have three options.

**A. Walk back out (free).** Go back to the arch you fell in through (Hold I's entrance) and step
through. You're set down standing in your ring, and the Upper Half rings bright for your homecoming.
No fight, no cost. **Everything you found comes with you.**

**B. Break the gate homeward (a fight).** At the gatehouse at the far end of the hold, **strike the
bell in the arch**. This starts a **Holdout**: stay within 12 blocks of the arch while waves of dead
rise out of the floor, once per toll. When the last toll sounds, the gate turns blue and points
home. You have 15 seconds to step through. Same reward as walking out, but quicker, with a climax.

**C. Go deeper.** Win the gatehouse's **objective** (put down the gatekeepers, or hold the gate) and
the bars lift like a portcullis. Step through and fall into the next kingdom. At tier 1, the second
hold ends in **the Throne**. See [Kings, Seals and Tiers](Kings-Seals-and-Tiers.md).

> **Important:** Walking out is **final for tonight's delve**. Once you've gone home, that ring won't
> let you back in. Your friends below can carry on without you.

## Step 9: Sell your haul

Back at the hearth, hold your kingdom treasures out to **the Assayer** and right-click. They're bought
on the spot for **Crowns**, which go into your **purse**. Spend Crowns at **the Provisioner** for food,
arrows and torches, and at other factors once they arrive. See [Crowns and Factors](Crowns-and-Factors.md).

## If you fall below

If you die, you **wake at the hearth**. You keep everything you brought down. Everything you
*found* stays below as **remains**: your head on the ground where you fell, bones around it, your
name glowing red over it through the walls.

**You're still part of the delve.** You don't need to strike the bell again: your ring's shaft stays
open, so walk back in and fall. You land in Hold I and can pass through every Waygate your party has
already opened. Find your bones and right-click the head to get your things back. The Waking kept climbing while you were gone, though. See
[Death, Remains and Dawn](Death-Remains-and-Dawn.md).

---

## First-delve checklist

- [ ] Get the kit from the Provisioner and put the armour on
- [ ] Strike a tier 1 ring and listen to the knock
- [ ] Fall in and watch your hearts fill
- [ ] Put down a few dead and see the heart particles
- [ ] Clear a room until *"The hall is quiet,"* then open its hoard
- [ ] Work an ore vein and read what it gave you
- [ ] Find the gatehouse and look through the barred arch at the next kingdom's rock
- [ ] Walk back out, or strike the arch bell and hold out
- [ ] Sell your haul to the Assayer

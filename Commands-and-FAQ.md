# Commands and FAQ

## Player commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/delve` | Inside a delve: your tier, hold, band and time remaining. Outside: prints the wall's three couplets |
| `/delve leave` | Only works at Hold I's entrance arch. Anywhere else it explains the Holdout |
| `/delve unstick` | Returns you to the door of the room you're in. 60-second cooldown |

Everything else is done **in the world**: strike bells, right-click factors, open hoards, walk
through arches. There's no menu.

## Admin commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/delve admin start <tier> [seed]` | start a delve without a ring |
| `/delve admin band <n>` | force the Waking to a band |
| `/delve admin end` | force-end your delve |
| `/delve admin skip` | jump to the door of this hold's last room (Throne or gatehouse) |
| `/delve admin factor <name>` | force a factor's arrival |
| `/delve admin reload` | re-read the config |

## FAQ

**Do I need mods or a resource pack?**
No. Vanilla Minecraft: Java Edition. Everything is done with vanilla blocks, sounds, particles and
mobs.

**Can I play solo?**
Yes. Everything scales with party size, and tier 1 is built around one player in the starter kit.

**How long is a delve?**
As long as you like, up to 90 minutes. Fifteen minutes is a complete delve of Hold I. A tier-1 Throne
takes 25–45 minutes.

**Is there a timer forcing me out?**
Only dawn at 90 minutes. Before that, staying longer just makes the dead stronger (the Waking).

**Why can't I heal?**
Nothing below is alive to give your breath back. Put down the dead, and their Breath returns to you.
Potions and golden apples still work.

**Why can't I place or break blocks?**
The halls are held exactly as they were the night they sank. Only ore can be worked, and only once.

**I mined diamond ore and got something called "Black Steel." Where are my diamonds?**
Ore below never gives vanilla ore. Each kingdom's rock gives its own treasures. Sell them to the
Assayer, or keep them. Valley diamonds are still on the surface.

**Why won't the chest let me put things in?**
Nothing found below can go into any container below. The dead would only take it back. Carry it, or
pass it to a party member.

**I died. Is my stuff gone?**
What you brought is safe. What you found is in your **remains** where you fell. Go back down through
your ring and right-click your head to get it. Dawn takes it if you don't.

**I walked out and my friend is still below. Can I rejoin?**
No. Walking out is final for that delve. If you'd died instead, you could fall back in.

**My friend logged on late. Can they join?**
Yes. Your ring stays open the whole delve. They fall in at Hold I and catch up through the gates you
opened.

**The ring just made a dull toll and won't open.**
It's either taken by another party, you've already walked out of its delve, or it's set to a tier you
don't have the Seal for. Sneak-strike to change the tier, or try another ring.

**Why is the same king back?**
Nothing below dies. You put him down, and he lies there until the gate closes. When the next opens,
he rises.

**Why is there no Nether or End?**
The only fire left in the world burns under the Cairn. The delve *is* the progression. Brewing
ingredients and netherite come up from the holds.

**Can I buy gear with Crowns?**
No. Crowns buy provisions: food, potions, arrows, stone. Heirlooms, Seals and tiers are never for
sale.

**What happens if the server crashes mid-delve?**
Everything you were carrying becomes yours, deliberately. The delve ends and you start at your ring.

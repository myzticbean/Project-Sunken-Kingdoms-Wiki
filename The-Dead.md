# The Dead

> *Nothing below is alive, so nothing below can die. What you put down lies where it fell until the
> gate closes, and rises where it lay when the next one opens.*

Everything hostile below is one of **the dead**. They can't die, because they were never alive. You **put them down**.

---

## They rise, they never appear

The dead lie under their own floors. When they come for you, they come **up out of the stone**: a
head breaks the ground, then shoulders, then a body, with soil kicking up around it and a low
rumbling groan. Only then do they turn and move.

| Who | How long to rise |
|---|---|
| common dead | about 2 seconds |
| wight | about 3 seconds, louder, more soil |
| Oathbound | about 4 seconds |
| a king | about 5 seconds |

That beat is **your warning**. A dead that takes two seconds to stand up is one you can back away
from. Nothing ever pops into existence at your elbow.

Ordinary room dead are placed further away, 16 to 40 blocks from you, so you mostly meet them
already standing.

## They keep their halls

Each dead belongs to **a room**. It won't chase you far from it.

- Lure one about **40 blocks** from its spot (roughly one room's width) and it **forgets the chase**
  and **sinks back into the floor**: the rise played backwards.
- If it's actually got you in reach, it will follow up to **16 blocks** further first, so nothing
  vanishes mid-swing.

You can kite a wight to a door, but not across the hold. Fights stay in the room they started in.

## They sleep

When nobody has been near a room for a while (about 64 blocks away), its dead **sink back under the
floor** and the room remembers how many there were and what marks they bore. Come back and they rise
again, exactly as you left them.

A room **refills** only after everyone has been gone from it for a few minutes and its original dead
are gone. A room you've cleared stays clear while you're in it. *"The hall is quiet"* means something.

## Every dead has a name

The dead aren't "Zombie" and "Skeleton." Each kingdom has its own names for its own dead, shown over
every one that stands:

| Kingdom | Common dead | Wight |
|---|---|---|
| The Greenwood | Rootbound, Poolshade, Thornspinner, Rootbiter | Rooted Thane |
| The Stonemark | Hollow Mason, Wallward, Stonemite | Hollow Warden |
| The Hush | Hushbone, Mute, Listener | Silent Herald |
| The Ashen Hold | Cinderbone, Emberwisp, Slagborn, Ashboar | Ashen Housecarl |

A mark goes before the name (*Fleet Thornspinner*). If you fall, the death message names the dead
that put you down.

**No creepers, anywhere.** **No baby zombies.** **Common dead drop nothing.** The halls' loot is in
the hoards and veins, not in rotten flesh.

## Marks

> *A king's household wore his marks in life and keeps them in death.*

Any dead may bear **one mark**: a power, a name prefix, and a coloured outline. The chance is 5% per
band of the Waking, so at band 0 nothing is marked and at band 4 one in five is.

| Mark | Effect | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| **Fleet** | Speed II | aqua |
| **Ironbound** | Resistance I, wears an iron helmet | grey |
| **Unresting** | Regeneration I | green |
| **Wrathful** | Strength I, but less health | red |
| **Shadowed** | Invisible. A smudge of smoke where it stands, and an outline whenever the bell tolls | purple |

The same Rootbound becomes a different fight depending on what it bears. Marked dead return
**1.5 hearts** of Breath instead of 1.

## Wights

A **wight** is an elite dead:

- **glowing**, visible through rock, so you can see it coming or choose to route around it
- a **house name**, like *Ceolred the Hollow Warden*
- **always marked**
- larger (1.2× size), with scaled health and damage
- **always drops a kingdom treasure**
- returns **4 hearts** of Breath

Wights become more common the higher the Waking and the deeper the hold. By band 3 in a third hold,
most rooms have one.

## The Hush's dead are deaf

In the Hush, the dead barely notice you unless you're within about 8 blocks, and they're **silent**
until they do. The **sculk** hears for them. See [The Kingdoms: the Hush](The-Kingdoms.md#the-hush).

## The Oathbound

> *The Oathbound, the kings' sworn knights, answer the bell.*

When you strike a gate's bell to break it homeward (a **Holdout**), the deeper tiers send the
Oathbound with the waves:

| Oathbound | What it is | Appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Oathbound Knight** | iron golem, hostile | tier 2+, band 3+ |
| **King's Hound** | ravager | joins the Knight at tier 3+, band 4+ |
| **The Host** | two Knights and a Hound | band 5 |

Tier 1 **never** has Oathbound, at any band. Each returns **10 hearts** when put down.

## Room events

As the Waking rises, rooms can surprise you:

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| **The dead rise** | a burst of dead behind you, and two seconds of darkness |
| **A wight and its retinue** | a wight with three escorts |
| **The candles gutter** *(planned)* | the room's lights go out for a minute |
| **The vault comes down** *(planned)* | pointed dripstone falls from the ceiling |

## How the dead scale

| Gets harder with... | What changes |
|---|---|
| **the Waking band** | health, damage, speed, how many, wight and mark chance, events |
| **the hold you're in** | more dead per room, more wights |
| **party size** | +50% health per extra member, capped at 3.5× |

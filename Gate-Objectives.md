# Gate Objectives

> *Every Waygate is sworn shut by its gatekeepers and barred with iron.*

Each hold's gatehouse has **one objective**: the forced fight or task between you and the next
kingdom. Everywhere else in a hold, fighting is optional. Here it isn't, if you want to go deeper.

When you step into the gatehouse, a **title** names the objective, and a **purple bar** keeps count.

## Slay the Gatekeepers

> **Slay the Gatekeepers** (red)
> *Put down 9 of the dead*

Put down a set number of dead **inside the gatehouse**. They rise out of the floor a few at a time
and keep coming until you've hit the count.

The count goes up with the hold number, the Waking band, the tier, and your party size. Expect about
4 in a solo tier-1 Hold I at band 0, and a good deal more deeper down.

## Hold the Gate

> **Hold the Gate** (aqua)
> *Stand in the ring for 45s*

Stay within **12 blocks of the threshold** (the floor under the arch's bell) while a charge fills.
Waves of dead rise around you. **Step out of the radius and the charge pauses.** Nothing else
pauses: the clock keeps running.

The charge lasts longer the higher the band, the deeper the hold, and the higher the tier.

## Either way

- A live objective is **loud**. It can wake dead from other rooms of the hold and walk them in.
- Win it and **the bars rise** course by course, the soul fires light, and the arch fills with a
  gateway to the next kingdom. Step through and fall.
- Crossing adds **+10 minutes to the Waking**. The next hold starts more awake than this one.

## Changing your mind

At any point, even mid-objective, you can **strike the arch's bell** instead. That drops the
objective and starts a **Holdout** to go home. See [Getting Out](Getting-Out.md). It can't be taken
back: the hold gives up the way onward for good.

# The Sunken Kingdoms

> *Strike the bell above a ring,*
> *and fall to the halls of a sunken king.*
> *Below, the arch bell tolls you down;*
> *a broken gate tolls you home to town.*
> *What you bear up is yours alone;*
> *what you fall with waits beside your bones.*
>
> — the wall at the hearth, Bellmouth

**Project Sunken Kingdoms** is a procedurally generated dungeon crawler for Minecraft: Java Edition. It
runs as a PaperMC server plugin. You play with the **vanilla client**: no mods, no resource pack,
nothing to install.

You spawn in **Bellmouth**, a town at the foot of a mountain called **the Cairn**. Under the Cairn lie
kingdoms that broke an ancient oath and were sunk into the earth. You strike a bell, the ground opens,
and you fall in. Each run (a **delve**) takes you down through a chain of fallen kingdoms. The layout is
new every time. You fight the dead who still keep the halls, rob or earn their hoards, and decide at
every gate whether to go deeper or fight your way home with what you are carrying.

---

## The game in sixty seconds

1. **Ring a bell, fall in.** In the Mouth of the Cairn, strike a bell over a ring. The bell below
   answers, the ground cracks open, and you fall into the first **hold** (one sunken kingdom).
2. **The halls give you breath.** You arrive with **ten extra hearts**. Nothing heals on its own down
   there. The only way to get health back is to put the dead down: each one you finish returns some
   of the Breath.
3. **Explore freely.** Every tunnel in a hold is open. Sneak past the dead, fight them, or rob their
   hoards while they still stand guard. Robbing is faster, but it wakes the hold.
4. **The dead wake up.** A clock called **the Waking** rises the longer you stay and the greedier you
   are. A bell tolls each time it climbs. More dead, tougher dead, elites that glow through the rock.
5. **Every gate is a choice.** Each hold ends in a gatehouse with a barred **Waygate**. Win its
   objective to cross deeper into another kingdom, or strike its bell and **hold out** against waves
   until it opens a way home.
6. **Reach the Throne.** The deepest hold ends in a king's crypt. Put the king down and every delver
   present presses a **Seal** from his signet. That unlocks the next, deeper tier.
7. **Bring it home.** Anything found below belongs to the kingdoms until you walk it out. Die and it
   waits as **remains** where you fell. Make it out and the **factors** in town buy it for **Crowns**,
   which buy food and potions for tomorrow's delve.

**Nothing is a ticket and nothing is a tax.** Entry is free. You never pay to play. All you ever spend
is time, breath and nerve.

## Three ideas the whole game is built on

**1. You fall in, you climb out.** You never walk into a sunken kingdom. Every way down is a drop:
the ground opens and you drift down into a lit cavern. Every way home is something you fight for.
Descent happens *to* you. The way back is something *you* do.

**2. Health is the timer.** There is no countdown pushing you out. Instead your hearts don't come
back unless you earn them off the dead. Fight carefully and you go deep. Rush and rob and you leave
early, at half health, with a full bag. Both are good nights.

**3. Nothing below is alive, so nothing below dies.** The dead are only ever *put down*. They lie
where they fell until the gate closes and rise again when the next one opens. That is why the same
king waits on his throne tomorrow.

# The Waking

> *The dead sleep lightly. A broken gate wakes the hold below. Lingering wakes them slowly. The bell
> tolls as they rise.*

The Waking is the game's **difficulty clock**. It's not a countdown to failure. It doesn't push you
out. It makes the halls **harder** the longer and greedier you are.

## The bar

At the top of your screen is a bossbar with **six segments**, one per band. It reads as a state, not a
timer. Within a band, the bar fills toward the next one.

| Band | Name | Bar colour |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Sleeping** | white |
| 1 | **Stirring** | green |
| 2 | **Waking** | yellow |
| 3 | **Risen** | red |
| 4 | **Marching** | red |
| 5 | **The Host** | purple |

## What moves the clock

There is **one clock per delve**, shared by everyone in it. It starts when the **first delver lands**,
not when the bell is struck.

| What | Adds |
|---|---|
| Time passing | 1 minute per minute. **A new band every 15 minutes** |
| Breaking a Waygate **downward** (crossing into the next hold) | **+10 minutes** |
| **Robbing** a watched hoard | **+5 minutes** |
| A Hush shrieker's first cry | **+1 minute** |

**Nothing ever lowers it.** Walking back up doesn't calm the dead.

Rough guide: a delver who rushes Hold I without robbing is still Sleeping when they leave. Rob three
hoards and cross one gate and you're already at **Waking** (band 2) before you've been down 10 minutes.

## What the band changes

The band drives **danger only**:

- the dead's **health, damage and speed**
- how many dead there are, and how fast rooms refill
- the chance of **wights** and **marks** (5% per band)
- the chance of **room events** (the dead rising behind you, a wight and its retinue)
- how many keepers rise when you rob (2 + band)
- how long a **Holdout** lasts and how big its waves are
- which **Oathbound** answer a Holdout

The band **never changes loot**. Loot is about depth. So there's no reason to wait around for better
drops, and every minute you spend is paid for in danger.

## The tolls

Every time the band changes, **the bell tolls**: once per band number, a second apart, each lower in
pitch the higher the band. At band 5 the bell is struck twice at once, slightly out of tune, so it
sounds **cracked**.

**Every toll shows you the dead.** All dead within 32 blocks of you **glow through the rock for five
seconds**. The bell was always rung to show the dead. Use that moment to read the room.

**30 seconds before** a band changes from time passing, you hear a faint **hum**: the bell
resonating quietly. That's your warning. Open that hoard now, or don't.

## How to read it

- **Sleeping / Stirring** (0–1): sparse dead, hardly any marks, no events. Read the cave.
- **Waking** (2): marks start to show up regularly. Wights become common in deeper holds.
- **Risen** (3): robbed keepers are always marked. Oathbound Knights answer Holdouts (tier 2+).
  Hush shriekers raise fresh dead.
- **Marching** (4): one in five dead is marked. King's Hounds join the Knights (tier 3+).
- **The Host** (5): everything at its worst. A Holdout here brings the Host itself: two Knights and a
  Hound.

## Design note: why a Waking and not a timer

Most dungeon modes push players out with a hard countdown. The Waking does the opposite: **the room
gets harder, the exit doesn't get closer**. You're never forced out (until dawn at 90 minutes). You
just keep asking yourself whether your hearts and your nerve will hold one more band.
